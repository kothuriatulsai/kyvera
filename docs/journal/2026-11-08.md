# 2026-11-08

## Step 30 — Slice B hardening: Proto Work ownership, two sequencing gaps (ADR 0015, revised)

Before merging Slice B, walked the review checklist by hand: ran the Stage 4→5 chain against the
dev database with real seeded users, then specifically probed a list of edge cases — a second
Merchandiser on the same Project who isn't the assignee, confirming twice, reassigning the
manufacturer after confirmation, uploading a PO before a manufacturer is assigned, completing a
still-queued Proto Work, and reconfirming/updating after completion. Two were real gaps, not just
things to verify.

### The real finding: `ProtoWork.assignedMerchandiserId` needed to be an authorization boundary

ADR 0015's original point 7 said assignment was "workload-tracking metadata, not an access-control
boundary" - any `MERCHANDISER` member of the Project could reconfirm the delivery date, post an
update, or complete the work, not just the one assigned. That was wrong: unlike Tech Pack
confirmation (where any Engineering member genuinely may confirm, there's no "assigned engineer"
concept at all), a Proto Work's assignment is a real one-to-one "whose job is this" relationship
the SOP's own Work Queue step creates. A second Merchandiser acting on someone else's assignment
is a real problem, not a hypothetical.

Fixed with `protoWorkService.assertIsAssignedMerchandiser` - once `assignedMerchandiserId` is set,
a `MERCHANDISER` actor who isn't that id gets 403 on reconfirm/update/complete. Deliberately
doesn't fire while still `QUEUED` (nobody assigned yet) - that stays the existing 409 ("must be
assigned before completing"), not a 403 that blames whichever Merchandiser happened to call it.
`ADMIN` is unaffected (never "assigned" to begin with). `GET` is untouched - layer-A visibility
still means any Project member sees the record, just can't act on someone else's assignment.

### Two sequencing gaps, found by actually trying them

1. `POST /proto-requests/:id/po` never checked that a manufacturer was assigned first - ADR 0015's
   own prose described steps 15-16 (assign) before step 17 (PO), but the code never enforced it.
   Now 400s without one.
2. `protoWorkService.addUpdate` had no `COMPLETED` guard at all - a chat update could be posted on
   work that was already done, the only one of the three Merchandiser-facing writes missing that
   check. Now 409s, matching delivery-confirmation and complete.
